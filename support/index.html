What should I include in a support request?

Include the software or game name, your device model, operating-system version and app or browser version. Describe what you expected, what happened instead and the steps that reproduce the issue. For Game Central, include the name of the game within the collection. Screenshots or a short recording are useful when the issue is visual.

Can I share a feature idea or feedback?

Absolutely. Choose the relevant app when contacting support and describe your idea, the situation where it would help, or the part of the software or game you would like to see improved. Feedback helps shape future updates, although a suggestion is not a promise of a release date or feature.
