Your games stay on your device

Game Central does not require a Drury Module account. Game progress, saved worlds, preferences and favourites are stored locally on your device so you can continue playing. The app does not send your gameplay data to Drury Module.

Game Central has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not include in-app purchases. It does not provide a Drury Module cloud-save or cross-device account service.

Apple services and backups

Your device may include app data in iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings. Apple may also handle App Store and device diagnostic information under your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy. These operating-system services are separate from Game Central’s local save system.

Deleting local data

Deleting Game Central from your device removes its local app data, including saved progress. Offloading an app can preserve its data. A separate device backup may still contain a copy; you can manage that through Apple’s backup and storage controls or your computer’s backup settings.

We do not hold a server copy of your saves and cannot recover a deleted local save for you. Contact support before deleting the app if you are trying to fix a problem and want to keep your progress.

When you contact us

If you email Drury Module, we receive your email address and any name, message, screenshots or other details you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request, investigate reported problems and keep track of the conversation.

Our support inbox uses Gmail, so email is also handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details or other information we do not need. Support correspondence may be retained while it is needed to resolve your request, handle follow-up questions or meet legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent.

Children and personal information

Game Central does not ask players to submit a name, email address or other contact information to play. Parents and guardians can contact us on a child’s behalf. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can review the message and handle any deletion request.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact Drury Module at camerondrurytas@gmail.com. Please name the app or website page your request concerns. We may need enough information to identify the relevant correspondence before acting on a request.

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date shown above. Third-party services manage their own privacy policies and settings.
